PawSort

Upload photos from one camera. For each photo we tell you if it is empty or which animal it shows.

We keep your photos on our server, because a person may have to look at a photo to check its tag, and because the next training of the model needs them. The model only gets a copy, and that copy is deleted right after the check, also when the check fails. A photo that shows a person is deleted at once, whatever the percentage is, and also when somebody picks person on the review list. A photo that we could not check is deleted too. We do not store names or contact details, and we remove the location data before we keep a photo. The small pictures below come from your own computer, not from us.
